`Manrope-Latin.woff2` is the Latin variable subset of Manrope previously bundled by the site's `next/font/google` build. It is now loaded with `next/font/local` so production builds do not require a Google Fonts network request. The typeface is distributed by [Google Fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope) under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/manrope/OFL.txt).
